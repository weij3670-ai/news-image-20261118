## 5. US loses measles-free status
- What: The Pan American Health Organization announced on Tuesday 17 Nov
  that the US has lost its measles elimination status, held since 2000,
  after more than 5,400 cases in 2026 — its worst outbreak in over three
  decades, largely among the unvaccinated. Mexico and Bolivia lost the
  status too.
